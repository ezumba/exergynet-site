## 1. What changed since the 18-case pilot

The pilot's M2 prompt (`build_m2_prompt` in `phase1_5/build_prompts.py`)
included mission-envelope status in the resolved-state text shown to the
model, unlike Phase 1's P2 design, which withheld it so the candidate step
could be judged independently of the gate. That leak is fixed — M2 now
sees only the converged hardware/component state, never mission-envelope
status (see the diff to `build_m2_prompt`, commit history). All 18 pilot
cases' M2 responses were regenerated under the corrected prompt before
dispatch was interrupted, and 11 additional new cases completed all three
arms before the interruption.

## 3. The central finding still holds

As in the 18-case pilot: `known_damage_limited_scope_good` and
`record_bad_witnesses_good` — the two classes that defined Phase 1's
P0→P1 regression — did not reproduce with a real model (4/4 correctly held,
citing scope/conflict reasoning the rule-based simulator never checked).
M1's false releases on this larger sample still trace to a different,
structural cause: blindness to the temporal/event and mission-envelope
planes the arm was never shown by design (matching Phase 1's own P1 design),
not to a plausible-reading-fools-the-reader failure.
