Token Mechanics

What $EXG Actually Is

$EXG is a transient internal accounting unit used within the ExergyNet settlement membrane. When a compute job is opened, the contract mints the precise equivalent in $EXG into a sealed Escrow PDA. This token serves as cryptographic proof that compute energy has been committed.

Upon successful ZK verification by the physical swarm, the Settlement Router burns the $EXG completely and atomically releases the underlying capital (30% to the node operator, 70% to the Protocol Treasury).

Important Clarification

What $EXG Is Not

NOT A SPECULATIVE ASSET

$EXG is not tradable on open markets. All liquidity pools from earlier epochs have been permanently removed. It cannot be purchased, held long-term, or used for speculation. Any attempt to treat $EXG as an investment vehicle is an entropic misunderstanding of its purpose.

$EXG exists only for the exact duration between open_job and settle_exergy. It is then mathematically annihilated.
