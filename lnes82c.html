N→K Corpus Scaling Validation

LNES-82C is ExergyNet's N→K corpus-scaling validation lineage. Its development ladder was executed on 4× NVIDIA A100-SXM4-40GB, while the 10M genuine holdout used a different WSL2/Windows retrieval host and is reported separately below. This page is the dedicated public evidence record for the LNES-82C lineage; the whitepaper carries a condensed summary of the same evidence.

Objective

Does active context grow with stored memory?

Test the relationship K = a + bN: whether the scaling coefficient b remains statistically indistinguishable from zero as tested corpus size grows, while task correctness is monitored alongside it. The evidence includes a ten-point development ladder spanning 32K through 10M nominal tokens, plus separately sealed 4M and 10M holdout evaluations.

Claim Discipline

What is measured, derived, and not established

Measured
  • Every mean/median/P95 K value and accuracy figure in the tables above.
  • 5/5 determinism (zero per-query variance) at both the 4M and 10M holdout points.
  • 0 per-query mismatches at both holdout points.
  • Retrieval timing recorded at the points where captured in source (P50 at every ladder point; P95 only at the 10M dev sweep and both holdout evaluations).
  • Corpus construction: 10M nominal target produced 31,361,425 actual characters (10,116,589 estimated tokens) via a deterministic, seeded construction function.
Derived
  • The 9-point (32K–4M) and 10-point (32K–10M, development data) OLS regression fits. Ten-point fit: b ≈ 2.79×10⁻⁶ tokens/token, R² = 0.0384, 95% CI [−8.59×10⁻⁶, 1.42×10⁻⁵] (includes zero). Sourced to the 10M development sweep seal, not the genuine holdout, which does not itself contain a regression fit.
  • Delta-K comparisons between adjacent corpus points (e.g. 2M→4M: +85.18 tokens; 4M→10M dev: −5 tokens).
Not established
  • Asymptotic behavior beyond the tested range. This campaign tests 32K through 10M nominal tokens only.
  • 100MB or 1GB corpus behavior. Not yet measured on this campaign.
  • A same-hardware 4M-to-10M retrieval-timing ratio. The genuine holdout's retrieval/harness host differs from the A100 ladder.
  • That Q_BEND indicates a system defect. All 950 holdout executions returned status=ok with valid output.
Claim Boundary

All results on this page are bounded to the stated model-serving configuration (nvidia/nemotron-3-nano-omni-30b-a3b-reasoning via NVIDIA NIM), the development ladder's retrieval/harness environment (4× A100-SXM4-40GB native), and the adversarial synthetic corpus construction described in the source evidence. The 10M genuine holdout used the same model-serving endpoint but a different retrieval/harness host (WSL2/Windows, tunneled to that endpoint) — this page does not imply the model itself moved between environments, only that the retrieval harness did, which is why retrieval-timing figures from that holdout are not treated as comparable to the native A100 ladder. Results do not generalize to other models, other hardware, or other corpus construction methods without separate validation.
