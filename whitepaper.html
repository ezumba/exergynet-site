Abstract

AI models are becoming more capable and more substitutable at the same time — new models arrive quarterly, and the cost of a single inference call keeps falling. What has not fallen at the same rate is the cost of everything a persistent, multi-step, multi-agent system must do around that call: reconstructing state, re-fetching evidence, coordinating across models and organizations. ExergyNet's first question is economic, not architectural — can a system reduce the resource cost of a validated useful task, measured against a workload's own optimized baseline, without requiring a different model or cloud? Efficiency alone is not the product: models can change, but institutional state must persist. ExergyNet is model-independent infrastructure built to hold that requirement, reporting its architecture, its measured results within their tested envelopes, and the research frontier that remains open.

1 · Useful Work

A measurement layer, before the architecture.

The correct denominator for efficiency is the resource cost of a qualified successful task, not raw tokens. On a single NVIDIA H200 with a Nemotron-class model, xLMP held staged prompt cost roughly flat at ~660–820 tokens as the underlying corpus grew from 8,000 to 285,000 tokens, delivered a 24.4-point accuracy advantage over a tested RAG baseline at equal evidence budget, and reached roughly 11.3× the correct-task throughput of full-context replay at each method's best compliant load. These are bounded, tested-envelope results reported as such — not a universal savings claim.

2 · Authoritative State

Context ≠ Memory ≠ Authoritative State.

Efficiency mechanisms reduce what a model is shown for a task; they don't establish what an institution currently treats as canonical. Authoritative state is the externally maintained record — version, source, provenance, authority, permissions, transition history — defined independently of any one model's internal representation. State-governance testing over 100 real procurement cases reduced false authoritative commitments from 8% to 0% while model judgment accuracy held flat at 84%: the surrounding system prevented unresolved evidence from becoming authoritative state — the model itself did not become more accurate.

3 · Model Substitution

Models can become interchangeable without institutional truth becoming disposable.

This is an architectural requirement, not a claim already fully demonstrated end-to-end. Execution-state portability is validated for same-process restoration and short fresh-process continuity; medium- and long-context fresh-process continuity is not yet achieved in tested runs (0/10 each), and cross-node portability has not yet been reported. The architectural objective and the current measured state are reported as two distinct things, never blurred into one.

4 · Administrative Boundaries

Interoperability without surrendering internal control.

Some requirements only appear once more than one administrator is involved — a vendor, a customer, a regulator, a cloud provider, none of whom can be asked to simply trust another's internal state. Each organization retains its own internal systems and its own authoritative state, bridged only at the interaction boundary. As the number of independent, heterogeneous participants grows, a shared protocol can reduce the bespoke integration burden each would otherwise pay separately — this does not imply every organization requires ExergyNet.

5 · Authority

AI can propose. It should not authorize itself.

Reasoning and authorization are architecturally separate. In a live test, a reasoning model partially complied with a prompt-injection payload; the deterministic validation layer rejected the request outright before any signed review could be produced — reproduced twice. The finding is bounded to one principle: reasoning and authority must remain separable. Authority originates with the institution using ExergyNet; a consequential action is constrained only where the applicable enforcement boundary is actually present and active — never a universal guarantee. 6 · Research Frontier

Stated plainly, not hidden.

Open frontiers, named because they are not yet true rather than because the architecture cannot eventually reach them: production-wide authority enforcement across every live route; true cross-process and cross-node execution-state portability; a physical-state observation primitive; and production-scale zero-knowledge verification integrated into the evidence-query path. A proposed comparative evaluation program — full-context replay, provider-native caching, optimized RAG, persistent/provider memory, ExergyNet/xLMP, and native optimization plus ExergyNet — is intended to measure xLMP's incremental value after an organization's own already-optimized baseline, not merely against an intentionally expensive replay architecture.
